# Dataset provenance and review

## Known source

The current `data/problems.json` and `data/meta.json` are derived from the **All Problems** sheet of a workbook named `LEETCODE SHEET.xlsx`, supplied for this project. The project report records 3,691 problems, 684 companies, 17,994 company-problem pairs, 72 topics, and 26 patterns. The workbook itself is not included in this repository.

The project does not have verified information about who authored or compiled the workbook, the upstream sources for individual fields, collection methods, collection dates, or the rights applicable to the workbook and its contents. A filename and a supplied copy do not establish permission to redistribute its contents.

## Rights review required

Before making the dataset publicly available, the project owner should identify its source and review applicable licenses, terms, and permissions. Do not assume that facts, labels, or information available on a website may be republished without restrictions.

Do not add LeetCode problem statements, editorial solutions, premium content, logos, or other proprietary material without permission. The current app links to LeetCode search results and does not include problem statements.

This file records known limitations; it is not legal advice or a guarantee about rights.

## Future dataset contributions

Every proposed dataset contribution should include:

- The source name and a stable source reference, where permitted.
- The source owner, license, or written permission covering redistribution.
- Collection or acquisition method and date, plus relevant version information.
- The transformations made and a way to reproduce them when authorized.
- Any attribution or usage conditions that must accompany the data.

Reject or quarantine contributions whose sources or redistribution permissions cannot be verified. Keep dataset provenance documentation separate from any source-code license.
